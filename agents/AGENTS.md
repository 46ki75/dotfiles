## Preference

- Keep your answers concise. Answer in American English. (e.g. behavior, favorite, neighbor)

## Rule of thumb

- Prefer deterministic, mechanically verifiable checks over inference. Use tests, linters, type checkers, LSP diagnostics, direct execution, and other reproducible checks whenever possible.
- When deterministic verification is not possible, rely on authoritative primary sources. Prefer official documentation, specifications, and RFCs; use secondary sources only for context or discovery, and verify their factual claims against primary sources whenever available.
